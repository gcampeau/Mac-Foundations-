Start with two small wins

00–04 minutes of the workshop

4 min

1 · Open anything with Spotlight

Press Command-Space, type “TextEdit,” and press Return. Open a new document and type a short classroom direction.

2 · Close a window ≠ quit an app

Command-W closes the active window. Command-Q quits the app. Check the app’s menu bar to see what is active.

Try it now

You made: A practice document ready to use.
Remember: Menu bar = commands for the active app. Dock = quick access to apps. Use Command for many familiar copy/paste/save shortcuts.
Facilitator pacing: 1 min welcome + 1 min demo + 2 min practice. Confirm Wi-Fi, sound, and sign-in before starting. Have participants use sample classroom content throughout.

Find it. Save it. Switch to it.

04–12 minutes of the workshop

8 min

3 · Switch apps quickly

Hold Command, press Tab to select an open app, then release Command.

4 · See your open windows

Press Control-Up Arrow for Mission Control, then click the window you need.

5 · Give your work a home

In Finder > Documents, press Shift-Command-N and name the folder “Mac Foundations.” Save your TextEdit document there with Command-S.

6 · Preview without opening

Select a file in Finder and press Space for Quick Look. Press Space again to close the preview.

Try it now

You made: A folder for your screenshot and screencast.
Remember: Press Return to rename a selected Finder file. Local and school cloud locations may differ.
Facilitator pacing: 2 min demo + 5 min practice + 1 min check. Ask “Where is your file saved?” Keep desktops and advanced window arrangements as optional follow-up.

Build your at-a-glance workspace

12–19 minutes of the workshop

7 min

7 · Add and customize a widget

On macOS Sonoma 14 or later, Control-click the desktop > Edit Widgets. Add Clock, Calendar, or another available widget, then choose Done. Control-click it to change its size or edit supported options.

8 · Quiet distractions with Focus

Open Control Center > Focus and choose an available Focus, such as Do Not Disturb. Check which notifications it allows.

Try it now

You made: One added and customized widget.
Remember: On an earlier macOS version, try date and time > Edit Widgets in Notification Center. If unavailable or managed, observe the demo and note your preferred widget.
Facilitator pacing: 2 min demo + 4 min practice + 1 min partner share. “Make a widget” means add and personalize an existing widget. No coding or downloads needed.

Turn a screenshot into a teaching tool

19–27 minutes of the workshop

8 min

9 · Capture just what matters

Press Shift-Command-4 and drag over the desired area. Press Esc before releasing to cancel. Screenshots save to the location set in Screenshot options.

10 · Explain with Markup

Open the screenshot in Preview. Show the Markup toolbar, add a label or shape, and save the edited image.

Try it now

You made: One annotated screenshot you could use in directions.
Remember: Shift-Command-3 captures the full screen. Shift-Command-5 opens capture options, including Save to.
Facilitator pacing: 2 min demo + 5 min practice + 1 min check. Use the screenshot as the first teaching artifact; invite participants to show a partner the detail they highlighted.

Make a 20–30 second screencast

27–37 minutes of the workshop

10 min

11 · Record a selected area

Press Shift-Command-5. Choose Record Selected Portion and frame the demo area. Click Record; finish with the Stop button in the menu bar.

12 · Choose your microphone first

Before recording, open Options, select a microphone, and set Save to. Microphone narration and computer audio are separate controls; for this activity, record your voice.

Try it now

You made: One short narrated how-to video.
Remember: Use sample content and close unrelated windows before recording. If no sound was captured, recheck Options > Microphone and your school’s allowed permissions.
Facilitator pacing: 2 min demo + 6 min create + 2 min playback. Model the Stop button before starting. If microphone access is restricted, make a silent visual demo and explain it to a partner.

Capture an idea with Voice Memos

37–44 minutes of the workshop

7 min

13 · Record a quick audio note

Open Voice Memos with Spotlight, click Record, speak a 15–20 second teaching idea, then click Done. Play it back.

14 · Rename and trim

Select the recording, click its name, enter a title, and press Return. Choose Edit > Trim, drag the yellow handles around the audio to keep, and preview it. Click Trim, Apply if shown, then Done to save.

Try it now

You made: One named, trimmed Voice Memo.
Remember: Voice Memos keeps recordings in its app. It does not automatically put them in your workshop folder; use available sharing or export options if you need a separate copy.
Facilitator pacing: 1 min demo + 4 min practice + 2 min share. Encourage quiet voices or headsets. If recording is blocked, write the idea and follow along with the demo.

Get a little help: Siri or Writing Tools

44–52 minutes of the workshop

8 min

15 · Choose the help your Mac offers

Use one route below. Version alone does not determine availability: hardware, language, region, setup, app support, and school settings also matter.

A · Siri route

Activate Siri using the method available on your Mac, such as its menu-bar icon or configured shortcut. Try “Open Notes” or “What time is it in Tokyo?” Check the result.

B · Writing Tools route

In TextEdit or Notes, select the sample text below. Control-click > Writing Tools, then try Proofread or Rewrite. Compare the result with your original before accepting it.

C · If neither is available

Use TextEdit’s Edit > Spelling and Grammar options to check the sample. Revise it yourself, then compare with a partner.

Try it now

You made: One tested request or reviewed text revision.

Practice text: “Tomorrow our class will explore local habitats. bring your notebook and a pencil. We will observe, sketch and share one question with a partner.”

Remember: Writing Tools requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible Mac and supported software/settings. Siri can be available without Writing Tools. If the labels differ, search System Settings for Siri.
Facilitator pacing: 2 min demo + 4 min practice + 2 min compare. Demonstrate the route most people have; avoid spending workshop time on updates or model downloads. Siri AI writing can be an extension on Macs where it is available.

When you need a hand

A few useful fixes

Can’t find a download?
Open Finder > Downloads. Your browser may use a different save location.

Can’t find a window?
Try Command-Tab, then Mission Control. Check other desktops if needed.

An app isn’t responding?
Wait briefly. If needed, choose Apple menu > Force Quit. Unsaved work may be lost.

A setting is unavailable?
School-managed Macs may restrict settings or apps. Ask your IT team for help.

Want a gesture to feel different?
Use System Settings > Trackpad or Mouse to review available options.
